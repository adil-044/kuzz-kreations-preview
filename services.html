Natural locs

Locs hairstyles

Natural locs are highly tweakable and extremely versatile — starter twists on short hair, micro locs, comb twists, restyles, and retightens with healthy-hair care.

How to start dreadlocks

The health of your hair is the most important part of your loc journey. With the right care and time you can two-strand twist starter locs on short hair, single braids on natural hair, or comb twist styles.

Micro locs & short styles

Short crochet micro locs and fine locs for detail and styling freedom. Healthy-looking locs start with proper care, patience, and consistency.

Loc styles & updos

Wear them up, wear them down, curl them, crimp them, and even color them. Whether your locks are long or short we can make your locs style look great.

Loc styles for men

We do locs with care, class, and passion — using the best products with integrity for your loc styles. Hair texture plays a big role in how to loc your hair.

Loc care

Castor oil hot oil treatment

Our castor oil hot oil treatment moisturizes locs, deeply penetrates all layers of the hair follicle, and with its high omega-9 fatty acid content helps prevent the scalp from drying out and supports healthy growth.

Best oils for locs: castor oil, olive oil, avocado oil & hemp oil. Herbal oil steam treatments encourage blood flow, hydrate locs, and help minimize dandruff.
